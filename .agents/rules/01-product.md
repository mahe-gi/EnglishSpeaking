# Product Rules (Always On)

## Core Learning Loop
The product exists to improve real spoken-English communication for job interviews and workplace conversations.
```text
ASSESS → AI PRACTICE → FEEDBACK → PRACTICE AGAIN → PEER PRACTICE → PROGRESS
```
Every product feature must strengthen one of these stages.

## Non-Goals (MVP)
Do NOT introduce:
- social feed, followers, DMs, public profiles
- video calls, group calls
- AI avatars, 3D characters
- grammar courses, vocabulary flashcards
- coins, badges, XP, certificates, leaderboards
- tutors, marketplace, IELTS/TOEFL coaching
during MVP unless explicitly requested.
